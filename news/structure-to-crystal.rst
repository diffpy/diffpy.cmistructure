**Added:**

* ``create_crystal_from_structure`` in the new ``diffpy.cmistructure.converters`` module, which creates a pyobjcryst ``Crystal`` from a diffpy.structure ``Structure`` and a given space group. It reduces the atoms to the asymmetric unit and raises a ``ValueError`` if the lattice or the atoms don't fit the space group.
